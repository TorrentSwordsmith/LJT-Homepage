I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group (Hong Kong University of Science and Technology), supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning and focuses on large language models: how they reason and can be trained to reason better, why they hallucinate, and what happens inside them when they do. I finished my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.

## Academic Background

* **Ph.D. in Computer Science, 2024–Present** — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), HKUST NLP Group. Supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
* **B.Eng., 2020–2024** — Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). Graduated in June 2024.

## Awards
